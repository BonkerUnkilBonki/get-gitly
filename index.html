Everything GitHub,
in One UI

The whole GitHub experience — browsing, reading, editing, shipping — wrapped in the clean cards, pill navigation and soft glows you see on this page. That's not a coincidence: this site is built with Gitly's own design system.

Repos, mastered

Your repos, starred repos and search-discovery in one place. Star, watch, fork, clone, rename, edit the description, flip public/private — and upload or edit files right from your phone.

READMEs, rendered perfectly

Markdown, badges, stats cards, images, embedded videos and collapsible panels — all styled like cards. Camo-proxy images are unmasked and big files fall back to the Git blobs API, so READMEs load on every network.

Commits with real diffs

Every commit across every repo, with green/red patches, per-file diffs and addition/deletion stats — plus a "commits today" counter that keeps you honest.

Issues & pull requests

A global inbox for your issues and PRs, with full conversation threads, replies, states and labels. Tap any thread and reply without leaving the app.

Alerts that respect you

GitHub notifications with unread dots, one-tap triage, and activity history that is never cleared — so nothing slips by while you were away.

Productivity, visualised

Contribution heatmaps, weekday bars and commit stats turn your GitHub into a glanceable dashboard — on the Home tab and on your home screen.

Gists & cross-device sync

Create and browse gists — secret ones too — and Gitly uses a private gist to sync your pins and preferences across every device you sign in on.

Multi-account

Work and personal accounts live side by side — switch between them in one tap, no signing out and back in.

github.com deep links

Open any github.com repo or profile link and Gitly takes over — it becomes the viewer, keeping you inside the One UI experience.

Built-in updates

Gitly watches its own releases and tells you when a new version lands — with a changelog viewer, one-tap APK download and optional auto-download. This page's app mock is v2.68.

The app, on your phone

Every screen is a One UI screen: 32-point rounded cards, frosted glass pills, accent glows. These mockups are built with the same styles the real app ships.
